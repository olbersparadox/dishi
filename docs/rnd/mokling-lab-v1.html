珊瑚 · differential growth

growing closed curve, fold where fed — cf. inconvergent, Floraform

A ring of points that elongates where ratings feed it. Repeated love in one direction has nowhere to go but folds, so a dim's evidence count becomes visible as buckling, not just reach. Dislikes suppress growth and pull the edge taut. Order matters: early loves shape the trunk the later ones fold against, so two users ending at the same vector still differ.

誠實條款 · honesty contract, preserved

  • Deterministic replay: seed + the exact rating history → the exact same being, every render. No hidden randomness accrues.
  • Fog dims contribute nothing. No phantom lobes, no phantom veins.
  • Growth is computed once per profile version (offline replay, same as replay.ts re-rating rebuilds); the live render only breathes on top and can never change identity.
  • Two render modes stay: live breathing canvas + static SVG snapshot from the same grown polyline, so version cards and share images can never disagree with the Taste tab.

參考 · research annex

3 parallel research tracks, condensed

Why hash-art and "unique pets" fail, and what works. Identicons are mathematically unique but perceptually identical (humans distinguish ~4-60 bits of visual information, so a million hashes land in a few hundred perceptual buckets). Niantic's Peridot had 2.3×10²⁴ genome variations and still churned: its uniqueness was rolled at birth, encoding nothing about the owner. Refik Anadol proves infinite gorgeous motion cannot rescue structural sameness. What works (Fidenza, Pokémon EV training, Tamagotchi care-mistake evolution): uniqueness that is earned, structural, and attributable — the form is a biography.

Algorithm survey verdicts (differential growth, space colonization, reaction-diffusion, Lenia, DLA, L-systems, physarum, metaballs, flow fields): the exact composite prototyped above — venation skeleton inside a differentially-grown margin — was independently rated "the most organism-like combination available at this perf budget." Space colonization is the strongest single algorithm: cheapest (cost per EVENT, zero per frame), perfectly deterministic (no transcendental-precision drift across engines), and its path dependence is honest by construction (veins consume attractors permanently — early loves claim territory late ones cannot). Lenia rejected: most parameter points are dead organisms, and every user must get a living one. Reaction-diffusion only as a baked texture. Physarum is the highest-ceiling someday option, GPU-only. Key references: inconvergent on generative growth · Runions, space colonization · Nervous System, Floraform · Karl Sims, reaction-diffusion · Sage Jenson, physarum · Jason Webb, 2D differential growth.

Traps the precedents warn about: no guilt, decay, death, or streak pressure (Finch beat the Tamagotchi lineage by being guilt-free; returning after a month should be a warm moment, the being settled and paler, rehydrating on touch). No rolled randomness. No tradability, comparability, or numeric level. No synthetic chores: Dishi already has the one true care verb, rating what you actually ate. And no stat dashboard on the being — the radar already exists one tap away; the organism is the poetic rendering, explained in occasional single sentences (「佢最近偏向濃味」), never charts.
